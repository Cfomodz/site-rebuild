Coaching

A thinking partner who has sat in the chair

Coaching came out of the consulting work. Owners would ask for a solution, and partway through it became clear they also needed someone to think with: someone outside the business who has carried executive responsibility.

Who it is for

  • Owners of small and mid-sized businesses who make every major decision alone
  • Managers stepping into their first leadership role
  • Technology and security professionals moving toward CIO or CISO responsibility
  • Founders preparing for a partnership, a new market, or a hard conversation

What you work on

  • Deciding what matters this quarter and dropping the rest
  • Delegating and automating so the business does not depend on your hours
  • Building partnerships and a business development habit
  • Leading people through change
  • Making sound technology and security decisions without a technical background
Formats

Three ways to work together

One-on-one coaching

Regular private sessions built around your goals, with direct feedback and accountability between sessions.

Leadership team sessions

Facilitated working sessions that get a small leadership team aligned on priorities and roles.

Coaching with consulting

Coaching paired with hands-on solutions work, so the plan and the person carrying it out develop together.

What to expect from Damon

Straight answers, practical next steps, and experience from decades of leading technology organizations. He will ask what you are trying to build, tell you what he sees, and help you put a system behind it so progress does not rely on willpower.
