Five red flags of a scam

  • Urgency. You are told to act right now or something bad will happen.
  • Unusual payment. Gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or cash by courier.
  • Secrecy. You are told not to tell your family or your bank.
  • Unexpected contact. A call, text, or pop-up you did not ask for, wanting information or remote access.
  • Too good to be true. Guaranteed returns, surprise prizes, or a new online friend who needs money.
If it happens to you

Stop, verify, report

  • Stop. Hang up or close the window. A real organization will let you call back.
  • Verify. Call the number on your card or statement, never one the caller gives you.
  • Report. File with the FBI at ic3.gov and the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
  • Age 60 or older? The National Elder Fraud Hotline is 833-372-8311.
  • Call your bank immediately if money has moved. Speed matters.
