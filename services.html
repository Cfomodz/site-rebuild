Signature method

The A.L.I.G.N. Framework

Most growth for a small business comes through other organizations: suppliers, referral partners, funders, agencies. A.L.I.G.N. is the five-stage method Damon uses to build those relationships and to rescue the ones that have gone wrong.

A

Assess

Test strategic fit, risk, and the balance of power before anything is signed.

L

Locate

Find the partners who hold the assets, access, or skills you lack.

I

Integrate

Line up operations, data, incentives, and meeting cadence.

G

Govern

Set the rules, metrics, exits, and escalation paths up front.

N

Nurture

Review the value created every quarter and adjust.
